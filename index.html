About Chai Katte

A katte is where the neighbourhood meets. We started Chai Katte to give students, families and office workers a clean, friendly place for fresh evening snacks that don't cost a fortune.

Everything is fried in fresh oil, made in small batches and served hot. Our mission is simple: fresh, hygienic and affordable, every single evening.
